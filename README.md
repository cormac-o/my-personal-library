Welcome to My Personal Library!

This is a personal project created by Cormac Ó Catháin (Cormac O'Kane).

Its purpose is to allow users to track media that they themselves have interacted with, add personal notes, annotations and ratings.

The idea transpired in mid 2026, I was in my final year of uni and thinking of ways to maintain some developing prowess. At the time I was picking up a hobby I had neglected for too long, reading, and before long I had read a number of books that I would love to be able to track my progress and review my thoughts, without having to tarnish a clean page.

It also came from an interest in lost media and the memories I have of old video games that I had played years ago and no longer have any access too, one in particular being Car Town Streets. It made me realise that the games I currently play may no longer be available in the future, and it'd be nice to go on to an app like this and re-live old experiences and forgotten memories.

At the same time, I expect to use this app to find new media to experience, although well beyond the MVP, I hope to include social aspects to this solution and enable recommendations (as suggested by my girlfriend Marika) based on previous reads and their ratings, genres.

This will be developed using PostGreSQL for the database communication.
FlaskAPI will be used to build the backend which will communicate with the database and the frontend.
AngularJS will be used to design the frontend which will present information to the user and retrieve data from the backend.

Flask and Angular were chosen primarily due to my experience with the technologies during university, while PostgreSQL was chosen by recommendation from MS Copilot (Don't crucify me) - I have experience with relational databases through uni and work, but have not used this technology in particular, so I look forward to building on existing skills, developing new ones, and overall becoming a better developer.
